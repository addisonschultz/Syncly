# Overview

### Overview

Syncly notifications help you catch work that needs attention.

They trigger when tasks change, deadlines get close, or someone reaches out to you. You can receive updates in the app, by email, or on mobile.

### Notification channels

Syncly delivers the same core activity through different channels.

#### In-app notifications

In-app notifications appear in real time under the bell icon in the top navigation. Use them for immediate visibility while you work in Syncly.

#### Email notifications

Email notifications use a daily digest by default. If you want faster updates, you can switch eligible notifications to instant delivery.

#### Push notifications

Push notifications are available on mobile only. They mirror your in-app alerts, so important updates follow you outside the desktop app.

### Trigger events

Syncly sends notifications for the events that usually need a response.

* **Task due date reminders** send at 24 hours and 1 hour before the deadline.
* **@mentions** send when someone mentions you in a comment or task description.
* **Status changes** send when a task you own or watch moves to a new status.
* **New comments** send when someone comments on a task assigned to you.
* **Project invitations** send when you are invited to join a project.

### Preferences and control

Manage notification settings in **Settings → Notifications**.

Preferences apply globally by default. You can then override them for specific projects.

* **Digest mode** batches non-urgent notifications into one daily email at `9:00 AM` in your local timezone.
* **Project muting** lets you silence an individual project entirely.
* **Independent toggles** let you control due date reminders and comment notifications separately.

Per-project overrides are useful when one project moves faster than the rest. Keep instant alerts for urgent work, and leave everything else in the daily digest.

### Example workflow

Maya owns a launch task due tomorrow and watches the rest of the project. She gets an in-app reminder 24 hours before the due date and sees a push notification on mobile at the same time.

Later, a teammate leaves a comment and tags `@Maya` to confirm a blocker is cleared. Maya receives that alert immediately, but keeps routine project updates in her daily `9:00 AM` digest.

If the project gets noisy, Maya can mute that project or adjust its overrides without changing her global settings.
